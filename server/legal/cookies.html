Cookies

Last updated %UPDATED%

In short

One cookie keeps you signed in. A few screen preferences are saved in your browser. No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to accept or decline.

1. The cookie we use

NameWhat it doesHow long
connect.sidKeeps you signed in after you enter your password. It holds a random session number, not your details. It cannot be read by scripts on the page.Until you sign out, or 7 days

This cookie is strictly necessary: without it you cannot sign in, so it does not need consent.

2. What is saved in your browser

The app saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage, such as light or dark mode, the last workspace you opened, which tips you have closed, and unsent drafts. They stay on your device and are not sent to us for tracking. Clearing your browser data removes them.

3. Other websites

Links to other sites (for example Facebook, Google or urbansociety.in) open those sites, which have their own cookie policies. When you connect an account, the sign-in happens on that platform's own page.

4. Your control

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you won't be able to sign in to Social-Nexus.

5. Google user data

When you connect a Google account (for example Google Business Profile), Social-Nexus uses the access you grant only to show and manage your business locations and to publish posts you approve. Social-Nexus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not let people read it except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires.

6. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp data

We use data from Meta's platforms only to provide the features you switch on: publishing, reading comments and messages, replying, broadcasts with approved templates, and lead forms. People who reply STOP are not messaged again. To remove this data, see Delete your data.

7. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Hosted-service data is stored with AWS and Microsoft Azure. Some providers above may process data outside India; where they do, we use providers with strong safeguards and only send what is needed.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Saved logins, access tokens, AI keys and mail passwords are also encrypted in the database.
  • Each business's data is kept separate, and people only see the workspaces they were invited to.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down.
  • Only a small number of authorised Radixile staff can reach the systems, and only to run and support the Service.
  • If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: while your account is active. After you close your account, or 12 months after a trial or plan ends with no activity, we delete it within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep.
  • Invoices and payment records: 8 years, as Indian tax law requires.
  • Security logs: up to 180 days.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves our backups within 35 days.

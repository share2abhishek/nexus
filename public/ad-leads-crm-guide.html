✅ How to Get the Verified Badge on WhatsApp

WhatsApp offers two types of verification badges for business accounts:
🟢

Official Business Account (OBA)

The Traditional Green Checkmark
  • Displays your brand name (Urban Society) in the chat header even if the customer hasn't saved your contact.
  • Free to apply via WhatsApp Manager.
  • Requires High Brand Notability (3–5 organic articles in top media publications like Forbes, Economic Times, TechCrunch).
Best for Established Brands with Press Coverage
🔵

Meta Verified for Business

The New Blue Checkmark Badge
  • Displays the official Meta Blue Badge across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Page.
  • Subscription-based via Meta Business Suite.
  • Does NOT require press notability! Only requires legal business document verification (GST, CIN, Utility Bill).
Fastest & Most Guaranteed Path to Verification

Step-by-Step Application Roadmap

1

Complete Meta Business Manager Legal Verification

Action Required

Open Meta Business Settings → Security Center and start Business Verification. You must upload:

• Official Business Name: Certificate of Incorporation / GST Registration Certificate / MSME Udyam Registration.
• Registered Address Proof: Electricity bill, telephone bill, or bank statement matching company name and address exactly.
• Domain Verification: Add a DNS TXT record for urbansociety.in in your domain registrar.
2

Configure WhatsApp Business Profile & Display Name

Configured (urbansociety2.0)

Your phone number (ID: 908833945656131) is connected to WhatsApp Business Account ID 2476543366144669.

• Display Name: Must exactly match Urban Society or appear verbatim on your website.
• Website & Email: https://urbansociety.in and contact@urbansociety.in must be published on your WhatsApp profile.
• Two-Step Verification: Turn ON 2-step verification in WhatsApp Manager.
3

Maintain High Quality Rating & Upgrade Messaging Tier

Green Rating Ready

Meta requires that your WhatsApp Phone Number maintain a Green (High Quality) rating and low block rate before granting the badge.

• Send approved opt-in messages to warm prospects (avoid bulk spam).
• Keep your 24-hour response rate high using Social-Nexus automated AI replies.
4

Submit Application in WhatsApp Manager

Final Submission

Once Steps 1–3 are verified:

1. Go to WhatsApp Manager → Account Tools → Phone Numbers.
2. Click the gear icon next to your phone number +91... → Profile.
3. Under Official Business Account, click Submit Request.
4. (Alternatively) Navigate to Meta Verified in Meta Business Suite to subscribe for the Blue Badge.
5. Review takes 2 to 4 business days.

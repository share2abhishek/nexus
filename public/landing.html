Questions

What happens after the free trial?

Choose the plan that fits: Individual for one business, or Agency for several clients. If you don’t choose one, your account stays: you can still see, write and export everything, but nothing is published or sent until you pick a plan.

Do I need my own AI key?

No. You can add your own key (many providers have a free allowance) or use the AI credits included in the bigger plans. You can switch any time.

Which platforms can it post to?

Facebook pages, Instagram (posts, Reels and Stories), LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky and Google Business Profile. WhatsApp Channels and Status get a ready-to-paste copy, because WhatsApp doesn’t let apps post there.

Is WhatsApp messaging allowed?

Yes, through Meta’s official WhatsApp Business platform. Broadcasts use templates that Meta approves, people who reply STOP are never messaged again, and you only message customers who agreed to hear from you.

Will it post or email anything without asking me?

No. Posts, outreach emails and suggested Google updates wait for your approval unless you deliberately turn approval off for something.

Can my team and clients use it?

Yes. Invite your team with the role that fits them, and send clients an approval link for the posts you made for them.

Is there a version that runs on my own computer?

Yes. The Social-Nexus desktop app for Windows keeps everything on your PC. Ask us about a licence.

How sending works
  • Nothing goes out without your OK, unless you turned on auto-approve for a campaign.
  • Emails are sent from the address in Settings → Email sending, a few seconds apart.
  • Each campaign sends at most its daily limit (set when you create it). The rest wait for the next day.
  • Follow-ups stop as soon as someone replies. People who say “unsubscribe” or “not interested” are never emailed again.
  • Every email ends with a line telling people how to opt out.
  • Addresses are checked for a working mail server before sending. Addresses guessed from a name (like firstname@company.com) can still bounce: check them in Contacts.
  • Pause sending stops everything at once; paused campaigns are skipped.
Social-Nexus doesn’t read your mailbox yet. When someone replies, add it under Replies → “Add a reply you received” so their follow-ups stop and AI drafts an answer.

Step 1 lets you send broadcasts and reply to chats. Step 2 lets customers’ messages come in, which Chats and Auto-replies need. The rest is optional.

Connect your WhatsApp Business number
Uses Meta’s official WhatsApp Business API. You do this once.

Where do I find these?
  1. Open developers.facebook.com/apps ↗, choose your app (or create a “Business” app) and add the WhatsApp product.
  2. In WhatsApp → API setup, copy the Phone number ID and the WhatsApp Business Account ID.
  3. For a token that doesn’t expire: Business settings → System users → add one → Generate token with the whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management permissions.
Only used by “Send test message”. Use your own number with the country code.

Meta sends each new message to this PC. Needed for Chats and Auto-replies; not needed to only send broadcasts.
Checking…
Meta can only reach a public https address. “Start public address” opens one for this PC while Social-Nexus is running.
With it, Social-Nexus only accepts messages that really come from Meta.
  1. In your Meta app, open WhatsApp → Configuration and click Edit next to Webhook.
  2. Paste the Callback URL and the Verify token from above, then click Verify and save.
  3. Under Webhook fields, subscribe to messages. Send a WhatsApp message to your number: it appears in Chats.

  1. In n8n, start a workflow with a Webhook node (method POST) and copy its Production URL.

Safety

  • Starts with Windows (in the tray), because posts only go out while Social-Nexus runs. It warns you before quitting or shutting down when a post is due. Turn it off from the tray icon.
  • Cloud posting (Agency and Full editions): tick Post from the cloud if this PC is off on a post. If your PC is off at the time, Radixile posts it 10 minutes later and deletes its copy straight after.
  • Backups. Settings → Backups: a backup every day (7 kept), Back up now, and Export a backup file protected with a passphrase — keep it on a USB stick to move to a new PC.
  • Saved logins are encrypted with a key protected by Windows. After a Windows password reset or on a new PC, connect your accounts again.
  • Recovery code. Make one (your name → Recovery code) so you can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in page. After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for a minute or more.
  • Automatic updates download in the background and install when you close the app, or choose Restart to update in the tray.

What Social-Nexus is

Social-Nexus is a Windows app that helps you run all your social media from one place:

  • Write posts with AI in English and 8 Indian languages.
  • Publish or schedule to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, X (Twitter) and Google Business Profile. (YouTube and TikTok are coming later; the AI tools can already write for them.)
  • Plan on a calendar, with review and approval for teams, and Indian festival reminders.
  • Use templates, a Brand Kit, hashtag research, UTM links, SEO tools, ad copy and email campaigns.
  • Manage several clients (agencies), each with their own accounts and team.

Your data stays on your PC. Social-Nexus is not a website. It only goes online when you publish, use a cloud AI or check your licence. You choose the AI: free local AI (Ollama), or your own keys for Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral or Claude.

Installing

  1. Download Social-Nexus-Setup-….exe from Radixile only.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": early versions are not yet code-signed, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise them. Click More info, check that the name is the Social-Nexus installer, then click Run anyway.
  3. Choose who to install for and a folder (the default is fine), then click Install → Finish.
  4. Open Social-Nexus from the Start menu.
Closing the window with ✕ keeps Social-Nexus running in the system tray, so scheduled posts go out on time. To quit, right-click the tray icon → Quit Social-Nexus. Scheduled posts are not sent while the app is closed. The app only accepts connections from this PC and adds no firewall rule.

Coming from the older "NEXUS" app? Your old data is moved to %APPDATA%\Social-Nexus automatically the first time you start. The app then offers to delete the old copy.

First run: creating the admin account

  1. Click Create Account, enter your name, email and a password (6+ characters).
  2. The notice "You're the first user — your account will be the Super Admin" confirms you are the admin.
  3. Click Create Account →.

The admin manages app-wide settings: social platform app details, email (SMTP) and users. Each other person gets their own login, posts and AI keys.

You may see "Set up your local AI" (3 steps for Ollama). Follow it now, or click Skip for now — use cloud AI →. A 4-step welcome box (Connect · Setup AI · Generate · Publish) can be skipped too. On Setup AI it checks for Ollama: if it's running and your model is downloaded, you'll see "✅ Ollama is running on this computer and llama3.2 is ready. Nothing else to set up."

Who can register: Admin Dashboard → Platform Config → Registration Mode: Open, Invite Only (codes from Admin Dashboard → Invite Codes) or Closed. There is no "forgot password" link: the admin resets passwords in Admin Dashboard → Users.

Working offline

After activation Social-Nexus works without internet until the Works offline until date shown in Settings → 🔑 Licence. When online, it checks in by itself and moves the date forward.

  • With 3 days or fewer left, a banner asks you to connect.
  • After that date, publishing pauses until you're online for a moment. Click Check now on the banner, or 🔄 Check licence now in Settings → 🔑 Licence (also handy right after renewing).
  • Don't move the PC clock back: the app will ask you to fix the date and go online.

Limited mode

Without an active licence: ✅ view, draft, AI writing, calendar planning, export and backup keep working. ⛔ Publishing, scheduling, Retry, CSV import and email sending pause. Your data is never locked. Already-scheduled posts wait; more than 6 hours late, they're marked Missed.

Moving to a new PC

  1. Old PC, while online: Settings → 🔑 Licence → Deactivate this PC. This frees your seat.
  2. New PC: install and activate with the same key.
  3. Copy your data folder across (see Backup).

Deactivate needs the internet. Offline you'll see "Deactivating needs the internet so your seat is freed for another PC." Nothing is removed and the licence keeps working; go online and try again. Old PC broken? Email support with the last 4 characters of your key and your purchase email. "Already active on N of N computers" means all seats are in use.

Reinstalling on the same PC

Just activate again with the same key (or coupon). Social-Nexus recognises the PC and gives back your seat. A coupon gives back the same period.

"This copy of Social-Nexus has been modified"

Program files were changed or damaged, so the licence can't be used. Reinstall from radixile.com. Your data is safe: it's stored separately.

PC with no internet

Settings → 🔑 Licence → No internet on this computer? Activate offline → enter the key → 💾 Save request file (activation.snreq) → upload it at license.radixile.com/offline from any device → bring back license.snlic → 📥 Import licence file. Paid keys only, not coupons.

Setting up AI: Ollama (free, private, offline)

Ollama runs AI on your PC. Nothing you write leaves the computer. It's free with no daily limits, but it's slower than cloud AI on ordinary laptops.

  1. Download and install from ollama.com/download. No account is needed. It runs in the tray.
  2. Download a model: in Settings → 🦙 Local AI (Ollama) → Pull a New Model type llama3.2 and click Pull (admin). Or open Command Prompt and run ollama pull llama3.2.
  3. Click 🔄 Check. The dot turns green and the model is listed.
  4. Choose the Active Model, tick Use Ollama as Primary AI, and click 💾 Save Ollama Settings. Or, per user: My API Keys → Local AI → Use Ollama as primary AI → 💾 Save All Keys.

Small local models are weaker in Indian languages. For Hindi, Tamil and other languages, a cloud key such as Gemini often gives better results.

How fast is Ollama?

  • The first AI request after starting can take about 30 seconds while Ollama loads the model. After that it's much quicker. After about half an hour without AI, the model is unloaded and the next request is slow again.
  • Ollama works on only a small number of requests at a time. If you start several AI actions at once, they wait in a queue and run one after another. Wait for each to finish; don't click again.
  • Faster: use llama3.2:1b, close heavy programs, or pick a cloud AI (Groq is fastest) on the 🤖 Model: bar.

How Social-Nexus chooses an AI

If you click a model on the 🤖 Model: bar in AI Generate, that exact model is used. If it fails, you see why, and there's no silent switch.

Otherwise it tries, in order: 1 Ollama (if on, running and the model is downloaded) → 2 Gemini → 3 Groq → 4 OpenRouter → 5 Mistral → 6 Claude. A busy provider is retried once. If Ollama is installed but your model isn't downloaded, the app tells you and uses your cloud AI. If everything fails, you see each provider's reason.

Tip: set up two options (e.g. Ollama + Gemini, or Gemini + Groq), so a busy service never stops you. If the Model bar says "No text models configured", add a key or start Ollama, then reload.

Connecting social accounts: how it works

Social-Nexus runs on your PC, so each network asks you to create a free developer app once. This also means Radixile never holds your social logins.

Easiest way: open Settings → 🔗 Social Platforms. Every card has a 📘 Setup guide with the same steps as below, Open ↗ links to the right developer page, and a 📋 Copy button for your exact callback address.

The steps are the same everywhere:

  1. Create the developer app on the network's website.
  2. Add the products or permissions for posting.
  3. Paste the callback (redirect) address. Click 📋 Copy on the card and paste it. Don't type it: it must match exactly.
  4. Copy the app's ID and secret into the card's 🔑 API Credentials, then click 💾 Save Credentials.
  5. Click Connect and allow access.

🔵 LinkedIn Ready · about 10 minutes

You need

A LinkedIn account · A LinkedIn Page for your business (LinkedIn asks for one when you create the app)

Steps

  1. Open the LinkedIn developer portal and click "Create app". (Open ↗)
  2. Fill in the app name (e.g. "My Social-Nexus"), choose your LinkedIn Page, upload a logo and accept the terms.
  3. Open the "Products" tab and add "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect" and "Share on LinkedIn". Both are approved straight away.

Common errors

  • connection failed — check your app credentials: paste the ID and the Primary Client Secret again.
  • Scope … is not authorized: check both products are added on the Products tab.
📘 Facebook + Instagram Ready · about 15 minutes

You need

  • A Facebook Page that you manage. Apps can't post to personal profiles.
  • For Instagram: an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to that Page (Instagram app → Settings → Account type). Why? Instagram only lets apps publish to professional accounts that belong to a Page.

Steps

One Page: chosen automatically, and Instagram connects too if it's linked. "Instagram Business account also detected ✅" confirms Instagram.

  • Instagram posts need an image at a public web address (https://…).
  • The login lasts about 60 days. Then Disconnect → Connect.

If Connect fails: the Facebook card (only Facebook) offers 🔑 Paste token manually (if OAuth fails). In the Graph API Explorer select your app → Generate Access Token → paste it → ✅ Connect with Token. It's short-lived, so use it only as a temporary fix.

Common errors

  • No Facebook Page selected: 📄 Select Facebook Page.
  • No pages found: reconnect and tick your Page.
  • Missing permission: pages_manage_posts: add the permission (App Review if needed) and reconnect.

🐦 X (Twitter) 🟠 Works with limits · about 15 minutes

You need

An X developer account · An X API plan that allows posting (check current plans on the developer portal)

Steps

  1. Open the X Developer Portal and create a Project and App. (Open ↗)

Common errors

Google Business Profile 🟠 Works with limits · about 20 minutes, plus Google's approval

You need

A verified Google Business Profile

Steps

  1. Open Google Cloud Console and create a project. (Open ↗)
  2. Request access to the Google Business Profile APIs. Google reviews these requests; it can take a few days. (Open ↗)
  3. In APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen, choose "External" and add your own Google account as a Test user.
  4. In APIs & Services → Credentials, create an "OAuth client ID" of type "Web application". Add the Authorised redirect URI: click 📋 Copy on the card and paste it.
  5. Copy the Client ID and Client secret into the boxes on the card, then click 💾 Save Credentials and Connect.

"Google hasn't verified this app" is expected for your own app: click Continue and tick all boxes.

Good to know

  • Until Google approves API access, posting returns a permission error.
  • In Testing mode Google ends the login after 7 days, so reconnect weekly.
  • Which location: with one location, posts go there. With several, click Choose location on the card and pick one; the card then shows Posting to …. Max 1,500 characters.
  • Post types: in Create post choose Update, Offer (dates, coupon, link, terms) or Event (dates and times), with an optional button such as Book or Order online. See Writing & publishing.

Common errors

  • redirect_uri_mismatch: paste the address with 📋 Copy into the Web application client. access_denied: add yourself as a test user. Fails after a week: reconnect.
  • “Google hasn’t given your Google Cloud project access…”: Google hasn’t approved Business Profile API access yet. Request it, wait, then connect again. “Turn on the My Business … API”: turn on the My Business Account Management and Business Information APIs in Google Cloud Console.

Writing & publishing a post

With AI (✨ AI Generate)

  1. Describe the post, with real details (prices, dates, places). The AI only knows what you tell it and your Brand Kit.
  2. Choose the platforms, tone, language and length, then click ✨ Generate Content.
  3. You get one card per platform. Click → Compose on a card to open it in Create Post with that platform selected.

Create Post (two steps)

  1. Write: type or paste the text, optionally add an Image URL, and pick the platforms.
  2. Click Save & continue →. The post is saved as a draft (it's on the Calendar now).
  3. Choose what happens:
    • Publish now: posts straight away (Social-Nexus asks you to confirm).
    • Schedule: pick the time, and optionally repeat it every day, week or month.
    • Send for review: it waits on the Calendar as "In review" until someone approves it. You can suggest a time.

Repeating posts (every day, week or month)

  1. Write the post and pick the platforms.
  2. Set First post to the date and time of the first one. Later posts go out at the same time of day.
  3. Repeat: Every day, Every week (same weekday) or Every month (same date. On the 29th–31st, shorter months use their last day).
  4. Ends: After N posts (2–365) or On a date (the post on that day still goes out).
  5. Read the summary, e.g. "6 posts, every Wednesday at 10:55 am. First: 28 Oct. Last: 2 Dec, then the schedule ends." The button shows how many posts it will schedule.

Each repeat is its own post on the Calendar (marked 🔁 Every week · post 2 of 6), so you can edit or unschedule one without touching the others. To end the whole series early, click Stop series in the Scheduled Queue or on any of its posts on the Calendar. Posts that already went out stay in your history.

Some platforms (X especially) refuse a post that is exactly the same as an earlier one. For daily or weekly repeats on those platforms, open a few of the posts on the Calendar and change the wording a little. A repeating schedule can run for up to 2 years.
Keep Social-Nexus running at your posting times. Close the window with ✕ (it stays in the system tray) instead of quitting, and don't let the PC sleep. They're marked failed with a message starting "Missed:", and you can 🔁 Retry to post now. Posts less than 6 hours late go out as soon as the app runs again.

Troubleshooting

Log file

%APPDATA%\Social-Nexus\logs\server.log (older: server.1.log, server.2.log). Attach it when you email support. It stays on your PC unless you send it.

"Ollama: not running on this computer"

  1. Open Ollama from the Start menu (look for the llama icon in the tray).
  2. Still red? Run ollama serve in Command Prompt and leave it open.
  3. Run ollama list. If it's empty, run ollama pull llama3.2.
  4. Settings → Local AI → 🔄 Check; make sure Active Model matches a downloaded model. If the model isn't downloaded, the app falls back to your cloud AI and tells you.

Ollama is slow, or AI seems stuck

The first request after start takes about 30 seconds while the model loads. Several AI actions at once wait in a queue and run in turn. Wait, and don't click again. Still slow? Use llama3.2:1b or a cloud AI such as Groq.

"API key rejected — check it in Settings → My API Keys"

My API Keys is under your name (top right). Paste the key again with no spaces, in the right box. Check it's still active on the provider's site (Claude needs credit).

"busy right now (503)" / "rate limit reached (429)"

The AI service is overloaded or your free allowance is used. It's not a Social-Nexus fault. Wait a minute, choose another model, or add a second free key so the app can fall back automatically.

"No AI provider available" / "timed out"

Add a free key in My API Keys or install Ollama. "Timed out" (30 seconds) is for cloud AI: check the internet or pick a faster model such as Groq.

Posts fail with "… rejected the connection … Reconnect"

The network login expired (Google Testing apps: every 7 days). Settings → 🔗 Social Platforms → Disconnect → Connect (accept all permissions) → 🔁 Retry.

"No Facebook Page selected"

Click 📄 Select Facebook Page on the Facebook card (Managers only).

"Port 3000 In Use"

  1. Click Try to Connect. Social-Nexus may already be running in the tray.
  2. Otherwise quit from the tray, end Social-Nexus in Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc), and restart.
  3. Close any other program using port 3000. Don't change the port: the social callback URLs depend on it.

Error when starting / blank window

Startup errors show the real reason and the log folder. A blank window usually recovers within 20 seconds. If not, quit and restart, then email support with server.log.

Scheduled post didn't go out

Was the app running (at least in the tray), the PC online and awake, and the licence active? Check ⚠️ Failed in the last 14 days in Compose and click 🔁 Retry.

"Missed: it was due …"

The post was more than 6 hours overdue, so it wasn't posted late. Click 🔁 Retry to post it now, or delete it. Keep the app in the tray at posting times. See Failed posts.

Licence: "Checking your licence…"

Normal for the first few seconds after start-up. Wait a moment and try again.

Licence: 🟠 Needs internet

The Works offline until date has passed. Go online and click Check now (banner) or 🔄 Check licence now (Settings → 🔑 Licence). If it says the computer's date looks wrong, fix the Windows date first.

Licence: 🔴 Not valid on this PC

Read the message. "…another installation" or "…a different computer": activate again with the same key. "This copy of Social-Nexus has been modified": reinstall from radixile.com; your data is safe. "Licence file is damaged": activate again. Cancelled or on hold: email support.

"Deactivating needs the internet so your seat is freed for another PC"

You tried to deactivate offline. Nothing was removed and the licence still works here. Go online and click Deactivate this PC again.

"Publishing needs an active licence"

Publishing, scheduling, Retry, CSV import and email sending need an active licence: Settings → 🔑 Licence. Writing, AI, planning and export keep working.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded to Radixile?

No. It stays on your PC.

Do I have to pay for AI?

No. Ollama is free; Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter and Mistral have free tiers. You pay providers directly, never Radixile.

Why do I need a developer app?

Social-Nexus runs privately on your PC, so it connects to each network with your own app. Radixile never holds your social logins.

Why does Instagram need a Business account?

Instagram only lets apps publish to professional (Business or Creator) accounts linked to a Facebook Page. Switching is free.

Does it work offline?

Drafting, the calendar, templates, Brand Kit and Ollama AI work offline. Publishing, cloud AI, email and activation need internet. The licence works offline until the Works offline until date in Settings → 🔑 Licence.

Will scheduled posts go out if my PC is off?

No. Social-Nexus must be running (the tray is enough). Posts more than 6 hours late are marked Missed; you can Retry them.

I reinstalled. Do I need a new key?

No. On the same PC, just activate again with the same key or coupon.

Can teammates use their own PCs?

Not yet. They sign in on the same PC.

I forgot my password.

Your admin resets it in Admin Dashboard → Users. Admins should email support.

Connect your WhatsApp Business number

You create and own the WhatsApp account at Meta. Radixile connects it to NexaDrip. Meta bills you directly at its own rates, with no middleman charge. Plan for 1–2 hours in one sitting.

Part A · Client does at Meta

  • Create a Meta Business accountbusiness.facebook.com, in your business name.
  • Verify the businessUpload registration or GST papers if Meta asks.
  • Add a WhatsApp Business AccountUse a number not already on the WhatsApp app, or agree to move it.
  • Verify the numberMeta sends a code by SMS or call. Keep the phone nearby.
  • Add a payment methodYour own card or credit line in Meta billing. Meta charges you for template messages.
  • Create a System User and access tokenGive it WhatsApp permissions. Share the token only through the secure step below.

Part B · Radixile does

  • Enter details in NexaDripToken, Phone Number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID under WhatsApp → Setup.
  • Set the webhook at MetaPoint to our server address and enter the verify token. Subscribe to message events.
  • Load your business detailsServices, process, timings and standard answers. You review them.
  • Submit message templatesFor confirmations and offers sent after 24 hours. Meta approves them, usually within a day.
  • Test with your phoneSend a message, check the AI reply and the inbox entry.
  • Train your team, then go liveReply, take over, and follow up from the inbox.

## Structural recommendations
- Sidebar for everyone (6 items): Home · Create Post · Calendar (Calendar/List/Failed tabs, absorbing Post History and the queue) · Email · Analytics · Settings.
- "More tools ▸": SEO, ASO, Ad Copy & PR, Hashtags, UTM Builder, AI Media Creator, Templates, Brand Kit, Local Files. AI Tools Directory moves to Help.
- Agency mode (a Settings switch, turned on automatically when a 2nd workspace is added): Clients, Members & roles, review/approval, workspace switcher. Hidden in Simple mode.
- Simple mode (default for solo users): no approval step, one AI setting ("Automatic"), Settings = Accounts · AI · Email · Licence (+ Advanced).
- Ideal first run: 1) your business (pre-fills Brand Kit) 2) AI check (auto-detect Ollama or paste a free Gemini key) 3) write the first post (pre-filled, festival suggestion) 4) connect one account (LinkedIn first; Skip = save draft) 5) schedule it, with a Home checklist ("3 of 5 done").

## Keep (works well)
Setup guides with Copy and honest availability badges; the welcome guide's Ollama detection; Indian languages and festivals; Compose character/image warnings and local-time scheduling; Retry; context help (❓/F1) with Print/PDF; the Licence tab; per-platform variant cards on AI Generate.

## Notes from the review session

- Test data added: one approved calendar draft ("UX test draft…"), one scheduled post (28 Oct 10:09), one retried failed post. All in the throwaway test database.

## Quick wins applied (2026-10-28)
- Compose defaults to Schedule (+1 h) for real (an init call forced "Post Now"); "Publish now" asks for confirmation; editor cleared after scheduling; clearer button labels.
- Calendar drawer: Retry for failed/missed posts; names instead of "User #n" ("you" for yourself); status labels ("In review"); "Untitled post" everywhere; schedule time pre-filled +1 h; friendly toasts.
- AI: coding/embedding models hidden from the writing model list; local models labelled "(on this PC)"; welcome guide shows "Continue →" when Ollama is ready; welcome platform buttons open Settings → Social Platforms (Pinterest instead of the unavailable YouTube).
- Wording: "Email message", "Show sample data (demo)" + honest empty state, invite "not emailed automatically" text, no "slug:", plurals on the licence tab.
- Accessibility/layout: --text-muted contrast, 11 aria-labels, header fits at 960 px.
